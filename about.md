# About

The Julia Language User Group is a monthly meeting point for Julia enthusiasts in Pittsburgh: a community of students, researchers and staff who learn and share around [Julia](https://julialang.org), an open-source language for high-performance scientific and numerical computing.

It is a place to ask questions, share techniques, and see how others use Julia in their projects.

## Who is it for?

- **Any level**: beginners are welcome.
- **Any discipline**: physics, statistics, biology, engineering, the humanities, and more.
- **Any topic**: numerical methods, data analysis, machine learning, optimization, visualization, package development, reproducibility.

## When and where

Monthly, usually on the **first Tuesday** of the month at **5:00 PM**, in Wean Hall (WEH) 2327, Carnegie Mellon University. See the [meetings page](/meetings/) for the current schedule.
